  • 公開：你的顯示名稱、頭像、個人簡介、你發佈的貼文與留言。Public: your display name, picture, bio, and any posts or comments you publish.

四、Cookie4. Cookies

我們只使用一個必要性 cookie，名為 token，用於維持你的登入狀態，有效期 30 天。它設有 httpOnly 旗標，因此網頁上的 JavaScript 無法讀取。我們沒有使用任何廣告或分析用途的 cookie。你的語言選擇儲存在瀏覽器的 localStorage，不會傳送至伺服器。We set one essential cookie, token, which keeps you signed in for 30 days. It carries the httpOnly flag, so page JavaScript cannot read it. We use no advertising or analytics cookies. Your language preference lives in your browser's localStorage and is never sent to us.

帳號存續期間持續保存。品味測驗與調酒紀錄各保留最近 50 筆，你可以隨時在「紀錄」頁刪除個別項目。刪除帳號時，我們會刪除你的個人資料與內容；已送出的聊天訊息因涉及對方的對話完整性，處理方式請見下節。For as long as your account exists. Quiz and cocktail history keep your 50 most recent entries each, and you can delete individual entries at any time from the History page. When you delete your account we remove your profile and content; messages you already sent are handled as described below, since they form part of someone else's conversation.

六、你的權利6. Your rights

七、安全性7. Security

全站以 HTTPS 傳輸，登入憑證存放於 httpOnly cookie。但我們必須誠實告知：本服務由小型團隊營運，聊天訊息未加密儲存，且尚未通過任何第三方安全稽核。請依此判斷你願意在此分享的內容。The site is served over HTTPS and your session lives in an httpOnly cookie. In fairness we should be plain: this is run by a very small team, message contents are not stored encrypted, and nothing here has been through an independent security audit. Please judge what you share accordingly.

八、年齡限制8. Age

本服務內容涉及酒精飲品，僅供已達所在地法定飲酒年齡者使用（台灣為 18 歲）。我們不會刻意向未成年人收集資料；若得知已收集，將予刪除。This service concerns alcoholic drinks and is intended only for people who have reached the legal drinking age where they live (18 in Taiwan). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below it, and will delete it if we learn we have.
